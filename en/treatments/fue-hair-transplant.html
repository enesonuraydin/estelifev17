HAIR TRANSPLANTATION · FUE

FUE Hair Transplant

FUE is an established technique in which follicular units are extracted individually from the donor area and implanted into planned recipient zones.

Free consultation →
Treatment timeapprox. 6–8 hours
Social downtimeusually 7–14 days
Initial growthoften from 3–4 months
Resulttypically 9–12 months
Treatments

Individual graft extraction for a naturally planned result.

A natural-looking result depends on careful extraction as well as hairline design, angle, direction and graft distribution.

Who is it suitable for?

Suitable for many patients with pattern hair loss or a receding hairline when a sufficient donor area is available.

Important to know

The number of grafts and achievable outcome are individual. Personal assessment is essential.
