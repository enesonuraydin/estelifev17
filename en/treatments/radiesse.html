CALCIUM HYDROXYLAPATITE · BIOSTIMULATION

Radiesse

Radiesse is a calcium-hydroxylapatite-based biostimulator that can be used, depending on indication, for contour support or improvement of skin quality.

Request consultation →
TREATMENT AT A GLANCEAll values are typical guidance only. Treatment time, recovery and results can vary individually.
Treatment timeapprox. 30–45 minutes
Downtimeusually 1–3 days
Effectpartly immediate; develops over several weeks
Typical longevitydepending on use, up to approx. 18–24 months
Treatments

Contour and biostimulation in one treatment concept.

Depending on dilution and treatment area, Radiesse can support different goals. In addition to an immediate volumising effect, it can stimulate the body’s own collagen and elastin production. Careful anatomical planning is essential.

WHO IS IT SUITABLE FOR?

Suitable for selected patients seeking contour support, structural improvement or biostimulation of skin quality.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Radiesse is not appropriate for every region. Expert anatomical assessment and discussion of risks are essential.
